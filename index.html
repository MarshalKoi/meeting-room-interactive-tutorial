Meeting Room Guide

Swipe to begin your setup.

How would you like to connect?

AirPlay Step 1

Open Control Center

Swipe down from the top-right corner of your iPhone screen.

AirPlay Step 2

Tap Screen Mirroring

Look for the icon with two overlapping rectangles.

AirPlay Step 3

Select "Room 101"

Enter the 4-digit code shown on the TV if prompted.

Browser Step 1

Go to URL

Open Safari or Chrome and type:
share.room.com

Browser Step 2

Enter Meeting ID

Find the 6-digit Meeting ID in the bottom left of the display.

Browser Step 3

Click Share

Choose "Entire Screen" for the best experience.

Mobile App Step 1

Download the App

Search for "RoomShare" in the App Store or Play Store.

Mobile App Step 2

Enable Bluetooth

The app uses Bluetooth to find nearby rooms automatically.

Mobile App Step 3

Join Room

Tap the room name that appears on your screen.
